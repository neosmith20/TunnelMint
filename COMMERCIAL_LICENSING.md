TunnelMint is made publicly available under the terms in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), currently the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

The public license permits the uses described in that license. It does **not** grant a general right to use TunnelMint for commercial purposes.

## Commercial Use

Commercial licenses may be offered separately by the TunnelMint Project Owner at the Project Owner's discretion.

A separate commercial license may grant rights that are not included in the public noncommercial license, including commercial use, redistribution, bundling, integration into commercial products or services, or other negotiated rights.

No commercial rights should be assumed unless they are expressly granted in a separate written agreement.

## Contributions

External copyrightable contributions intended for inclusion in TunnelMint are subject to the ownership and assignment requirements in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [`CONTRIBUTOR_COPYRIGHT_ASSIGNMENT.md`](CONTRIBUTOR_COPYRIGHT_ASSIGNMENT.md). This allows the Project Owner to maintain consistent licensing and, where desired, offer TunnelMint under commercial or other licensing terms.

## Third-Party Components

A TunnelMint commercial license can only grant rights controlled by the TunnelMint Project Owner. Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses and notices. See [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Inquiries

Commercial licensing inquiries may be directed to the repository maintainers. Do not post confidential business information in a public issue.
