A **Contribution** means original code, documentation, artwork, tests, designs, or other copyrightable material that You intentionally submit for inclusion in TunnelMint and that is identified as covered by this Agreement.

Unless required by applicable law or separately agreed in writing, You provide the Contribution on an "as is" basis without warranties or conditions of any kind.

Opening a pull request, issue, patch, or other submission does **not**, by itself, execute this Agreement.

A copyright assignment must be accepted through a designated signed or electronic process that identifies the contributor and the covered Contribution. TunnelMint should not merge an external copyrightable Contribution until that acceptance has been recorded.

This Agreement governs ownership of the covered Contribution unless the contributor and Project Owner enter into a separate written agreement for that Contribution.
