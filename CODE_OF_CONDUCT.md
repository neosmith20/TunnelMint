# Code of Conduct

WireHush welcomes constructive participation from users, testers, contributors, and maintainers.

## Expected Conduct

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, behavior, and technical decisions rather than people;
- provide enough context for others to understand problems and proposed changes;
- respect security-reporting and disclosure requirements;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, or intentionally disruptive behavior.

Technical disagreement is normal. Be direct when needed, but keep discussions productive.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Examples include:

- harassment or targeted abuse;
- threats or encouragement of harm;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments directed at a person or group;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- intentionally posting secrets, credentials, exploit details, or sensitive user data;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked that behavior to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.

Decisions should be based on behavior within project spaces and the needs of the project.
